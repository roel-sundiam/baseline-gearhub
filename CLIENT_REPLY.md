# Client Reply — Booking Info & Common Issues

Hi! Great questions. Here's a rundown of how the system works and what to watch out for:

## No Double Bookings
The app automatically prevents this. When a player goes to book a court, any time slot that's already taken is greyed out — they simply can't tap or click on it. And even if two people tried to book the exact same slot at the same time, the system will only accept the first one and tell the second person that the slot is already taken. So double bookings just can't happen.

## A Few Things Worth Knowing About Bookings

- **Can't book the past** — Players can't accidentally book a date that has already passed.
- **Cancellations are easy** — Players can cancel their own bookings. Admins can cancel or remove any booking. If a payment hasn't been made yet, the charge is automatically removed too.
- **Changing a booking** — Players can update their own upcoming bookings — change the court, the time, or any add-ons like lights or a ball boy. The system will still make sure the new slot they pick isn't already taken by someone else.
- **Pricing is automatic** — The correct rate (weekday, weekend, or holiday) is applied automatically based on the date, so there's no guesswork.
- **Courts are set by you** — Players can only book courts that your club actually has. If you have 2 courts, only Courts 1 and 2 will show up.

## Common Situations That Might Come Up

- **Player books but doesn't show up** — If someone books a court and doesn't cancel, that slot stays blocked. It's good practice to remind members to cancel in advance if they can't make it, so others can use the slot.
- **Holiday not marked correctly** — When booking, there's an option to flag a date as a holiday (which applies the holiday rate). If a player forgets to tick that option, they'll be charged the regular rate instead. Worth reminding members to check that when booking on public holidays.
- **Lights or add-ons not added** — Things like lights, a ball boy, or equipment rentals need to be selected at the time of booking. If a player forgets and shows up expecting them, you'd need to manually adjust the charge on your end.
- **Guest count not declared** — If a member brings guests, they're supposed to enter the number of guests when booking. Some might forget, which affects the charge. A quick reminder policy goes a long way here.
- **Equipment rentals (ball machine, rackets, balls)** — Players can request these when booking, but the app doesn't track physical inventory. If you run out of a certain item, you'd need to communicate that directly with the player — the system won't know the item is unavailable.
- **Last-minute cancellations** — Right now, players can cancel anytime, even right before their slot. If you want a cancellation cutoff (e.g., no cancellations within 2 hours of the booking), that would need to be a club policy you enforce manually, or we can look at adding it to the app later.
- **Forgotten passwords / can't log in** — Members who can't log in can use the "Forgot Password" option, or an admin can assist them.
- **Charges not yet paid** — Bookings are confirmed right away, but payments are settled separately. It's a good idea to regularly check the payments section so no charges slip through unpaid for too long.

Hope that covers everything! Feel free to reach out anytime if something comes up.
